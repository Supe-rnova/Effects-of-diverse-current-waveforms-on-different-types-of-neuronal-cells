## Why these models
* **Purkinje – Miyasho 2001 over Masoli 2015:** Masoli's model fires spontaneously but its
  own README quotes 4–15 min per 5 s simulation on an 8-core CPU – impossible for a
  batch of hundreds of runs. Miyasho keeps full morphology and dendritic Ca²⁺ spikes.
  Consequence: Purkinje is *not* spontaneously active in this model, so only STN needs
  the "native vs held" treatment.
* **CA1 – Migliore et al. 2018 (after testing three alternatives).** Candidates were
  scored on resonance (time-domain ZAP), firing rate and spike-frequency adaptation
  (1 s steps):

  | Model | rate @ step | adaptation (last/first ISI) | soma fR, Q | notes |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Migliore & Migliore 2012 (144541) | – | – | 8 Hz, 1.17 | Rin ~16 MΩ – unrealistic |
  | Migliore et al. 2004 (32992) | 72 Hz @ 0.3 nA | 0.97 (none) | 7.8 Hz, 1.13 | no M / K-Ca channels |
  | Poirazi et al. 2003 (20212) | 52 Hz @ 0.3 nA | 1.13 | 2.8 Hz, 1.05 | weak resonance |
  | **Migliore et al. 2018 (244688)** | **19 Hz @ 0.6 nA** | **2.9–5.8** | **10 Hz, 1.35** | fitted to CA1 recordings |

  Blocking Ih in the chosen cell lowers fR to ~4 Hz and Q to ~1.07 – the residual is the
  M-current resonance described by Hu et al. (2002).

## Files we wrote (everything else is upstream, unmodified)
* `stn/stn_build.hoc` – GUI-free builder (cell set-up copied from sample.hoc,
  demo runs/graphics removed). CA1 uses the published BluePyOpt template directly.
* `purkinje/purkinje_nogui.hoc` – generated by `fetch_models.py` (2 GUI lines removed).
* `cells.py` – common interface (`build_cell`, sites, channel blockade).
* `mechanisms.py` – compile/load helpers.

## Important
* One model per Python process (mechanism names clash, e.g. `CaT`).
* `h.Impedance` (NEURON's linearised impedance) does **not** capture the Ih gating in
  these mod files – it shows no resonance even in a toy cell where a time-domain ZAP
  clearly does. Resonance is therefore measured from the chirp/ZAP FFT only.

## Characterisation notes (Step 4)
* tau_m is the *apparent* somatic time constant (time to 63 % of the peak response to a
  ~3 mV hyperpolarising step). For CA1 (3.8 ms) and STN (6.0 ms) it is shorter than the
  passive membrane value (CA1: C_m/g_pas ~ 11 ms) because current injected at the soma
  charges the dendritic tree and resting K+/Ih conductances add to the leak; blocking Ih
  only raises CA1's value to ~6 ms.
* Purkinje: dendritic rheobase (0.72 nA at 100 um) is LOWER than somatic (0.85 nA) and the
  dendritic response is delayed bursting driven by dendritic Ca2+ spikes.
* STN 'held' condition: -0.182 nA holds the cell silent at -70 mV.
